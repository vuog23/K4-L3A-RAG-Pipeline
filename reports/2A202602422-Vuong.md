## Technical decisions visible in the implementation

2. **RRF combines dense and lexical rankings.** `rerank_rrf()` applies reciprocal-rank scores and the retrieval pipeline checks the best dense cosine score for fallback. This keeps fallback decisions separate from the fused ranking score; no measured A/B result is available to establish its impact.
